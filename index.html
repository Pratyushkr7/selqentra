Three kinds of supplier. Most lists can't tell them apart.

Directories and marketplaces mix them together, and many traders present themselves as manufacturers. We label every name on evidence, not on what its website claims.

Manufacturer

Owns the plant and makes the product. Registrations in its own name, its own technical documents.

Why it mattersBest price and spec control once you buy in volume.

Distributor

Resells named producers under agreement. Adds stock, small pack sizes and local delivery.

Why it mattersOften the right first buy for a new product or a small first order.

Trader

Buys and resells without a fixed source. Fine for spot buys, risky for a product you'll repeat.

Why it mattersBatch-to-batch consistency is not guaranteed.

Questions

How much will I save?

It depends on how many layers sit between you and the plant today. We don't promise a percentage because we haven't seen your quotes. What we promise is that you'll know who makes the material, so the next negotiation starts from facts.

Do you give prices?

Suppliers quote against a specific request, so prices come from the RFQ you send. The shortlist includes any public price signals we find, and the RFQ email asks for price, MOQ and lead time in one go.

What if my material is niche?

Some materials have only three or four real producers worldwide. We check before we start and tell you what to expect, so you pay for a complete picture, not a padded list.

Will you contact suppliers for me?

Not in the base shortlist, so your request stays private until you choose to send it. If you want us to collect and compare quotes, ask and we'll scope it.

Is my product idea kept confidential?

Yes. Your request is used only for your research, never shared with suppliers or other clients. We're happy to sign your NDA.

What if I only know the product, not the material?

Send the product, a competitor's label, the ingredient list or the TDS of what you buy now. We'll work out what to source and confirm with you before starting.

Where are the suppliers from?

Wherever the best fit is. For Indian buyers we start with Indian producers; for international buyers we cover India, China, Europe and the US unless you tell us otherwise.
