For buyers: where the savings come from

A list of suppliers is the means. A better price is the point.

Skip a layer

If the company you buy from turns out to be a trader, you can go to the producer or its named distributor and keep the difference.

Negotiate with a real alternative

A quote from a genuine second producer is the strongest argument in any price discussion, even if you end up staying with your current supplier.

Stop paying for bad samples

Testing material from resellers who can't repeat a batch costs weeks and lab time. Start with the companies that make it.

Keep the line running

A verified backup producer means one plant shutdown or one missed shipment doesn't stop your production.

Before you ask.

How much will I save?

It depends on how many layers sit between you and the plant today. We don't promise a percentage because we haven't seen your quotes. What we do promise is that you'll know who makes the material, so the next negotiation starts from facts.

What does it cost?

It depends on the material, the markets and how deep you want to go. We send the scope and fee before any work starts, and you pay only after you have the report.

How do you find buyers for suppliers?

From customs shipment records, product and regulatory filings, and the manufacturers' own sites. We identify who uses the material, at what scale and from whom they buy today, then the right person to contact. We never share one client's request with another.

What if my material is niche?

Some materials have three or four real producers worldwide. We check before we start and tell you what to expect, so you pay for a complete picture, not a padded list.

Will you contact suppliers for me?

Not in the standard report, so your request stays private until you choose to send it. If you want us to collect and compare quotes, ask and we'll scope it.

Is my request kept confidential?

Yes. It's used only for your research and never shared with suppliers or other clients. We're happy to sign your NDA.

Where are the producers from?

Wherever the best fit is. For Indian buyers we start with Indian producers and importers; for international buyers we cover India, China, Europe and the US unless you say otherwise.
